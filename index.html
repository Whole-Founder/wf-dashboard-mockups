  • SSI Warm Asset-Delivery Page: Wireframe v1 (F1, copyless + annotated)
    The next-level wireframe for SSI's warm-traffic email funnel, and the first page built to the new WF wireframe standard: no copy, no brand, just labelled slots. Every content box carries its slot type, a word budget, and the one job it has to do, and every section carries a spec rail (layout, fold budget, media, element counts, reference). That means the wireframe doubles as the copy brief, with the full slot table at the bottom of the page. Opens in a clean Preview view (the page and nothing else) with a page-overview summary above it; one switch layers on the Specs view (layout rails, word budgets, the job each box has to do), globally or one section at a time. Structure reconciles four sources (the Miro map, the client-agnostic standard, Natalya's AI pass, and the live build); the four places they genuinely disagreed are now settled, with each decision and its reasoning recorded on the section it affects. Self-checked clean at 375 and 1440. For Gray's structure sign-off and Natalya's copy pass; funnel suggestions go to #wf-funnel-cro via the button at the bottom.
