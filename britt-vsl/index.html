What Self Storage Income is not

Self Storage Income is not a brokerage, passive syndication fund, done-for-you deal service, or a guaranteed partnership with AJ or Britt.

What Self Storage Income is

Self Storage Income is an acquisition program led by active operators AJ Osborne and Britt Arnason for investors who want to evaluate, acquire, and own their own self-storage facility.

Built for serious acquisition intent

Is SSI the right next step for you?

SSI is built for investors ready to do the work of evaluating and acquiring a facility they will own. It is not for passive investors waiting for someone else to hand them a deal.
