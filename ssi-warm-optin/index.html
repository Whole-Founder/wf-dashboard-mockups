H22 to 5 words

Common questions

Signal the section without selling. This is the one header on the page allowed to be plain.

FAQ accordion · 5 rowsQ 6-12w · A 30-50w
How much capital do I need to get started?
Answer the money objection directly and with a number, because a warm list will not ask twice. State the minimum plainly, say what it is for, and name the situation where someone should wait instead.
How much time does this actually take each week?
Give the honest weekly number rather than a comfortable one. Separate the time spent learning the process from the time spent working a live deal, because those two are very different commitments.
Is this right for me if I own one facility?
Restate the fit question the way someone would actually type it, then answer with the situation rather than the job title. Point back to the two columns above instead of repeating them here.
Does this work in my market or my region?
Answer the market objection with a proof case rather than a promise. Name a member who bought in a comparable market and say what was the same about the process rather than the geography.
What actually happens on the first call?
Describe the call itself, step by step. Who they speak to, how long it runs, what to bring, and what happens if it is not a fit. This is the highest leverage answer on a booking page.

Five real objections in frequency order: price, time, is this for me, does it work in my situation, what happens on the call. Answer directly, using a proof case rather than a promise where possible.

Rules that apply to every slot

  • One CTA label family. The same words at header, mid-page CTA, final CTA and sticky mobile bar. No secondary button anywhere.
  • Cleared proof only. Any name, quote or number that is not cleared stays out. It is never replaced with a plausible substitute.
  • No em dashes. House rule, applies to every line on the page.
  • Budgets are structural. They come from the fold budgets in each section spec. Overrunning a budget moves the first fold, which is the one thing this wireframe exists to protect.
  • The four structural questions are settled. Decisions A to D are recorded in place on the page above, with the reasoning. Nothing is blocking copy except the client disclaimer.
How to read and use this wireframe optional

A wireframe locks structure, not words. It answers one question: is this laid out in the right order, with the right emphasis, to move this visitor to action? Copy, brand and real images are deliberately stripped out, because reordering a box is free and rewriting finished copy is not.

Two views

Full preview is the page itself: the real type at the real size, on real page spacing, scrolling without interruption. No section labels, no rails, no annotation. This is the view for judging whether the page works. Specs layers the build detail back on. Switch at the top, or open one section at a time with the faint button on its top right.

What Specs adds

  • A spec rail per section: layout, how many folds it costs, media, element counts, and the reference it was built against.
  • A word budget on every box: the length the layout is built for.
  • A job on every box: the one thing that piece of copy has to accomplish.
  • The decisions behind the four places our sources disagreed, recorded where they apply.

Who uses which view

  • Reviewer (Gray, the client): Full preview. Sign off on section order and emphasis before money goes to copy or design.
  • Copywriter: Specs. Every box states its job and its budget, so the page can be written without asking a question. The same content is in the copy brief below and in the .md shipped alongside.
  • Designer and developer: Specs. Layout, counts and fold budgets are the build contract.

Two conventions worth knowing

  • Tags. SWAP changes per variant, CONSTANT is written once and reused, CUSTOMIZE V2 is constant now and hand-tuned per video later.
  • Amber means blocked. Something we genuinely do not have yet, like the legal disclaimer. It is never filled with a plausible substitute.

The dark bar at the top, the view switch, the section summary and this block are all wireframe scaffolding. None of it exists on the real page.
