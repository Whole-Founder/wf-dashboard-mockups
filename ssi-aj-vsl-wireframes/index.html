H23 to 6 words

Questions before you book

Signpost the objection handling for the booking step.

FAQ 1Q 5-12w · A 40-70w
What actually happens on the call?
You start with a short application. If it looks like a fit, we set up a Storage Wealth Game Plan call, and before it you fill out a short readiness assessment. On the call you leave with a readiness score across your capital, financing, timing, and fit. Either way, you leave knowing where you stand. Bring your spouse or business partner.

Set the two-step expectation and the payoff, and invite the decision-maker.

FAQ 2Q 5-12w · A 40-70w
Do I need experience or a deal already in hand?
No. This is built for people buying their first storage facility, so we do not expect you to have owned one. You also do not need a deal in hand. The program brings you deals and the tools to tell what each one is worth, so you are not sourcing alone or guessing on price. What you bring is real estate or business experience and capital that is ready.

Kill the two disqualifying fears: no experience, no deal yet.

FAQ 3Q 5-12w · A 40-70w
Is this a broker who sells me a deal?
No. We are not a broker. We do not sell you a deal or take a broker's cut, and you own the facility you buy. What we do is work the deal alongside you: help you find it, tell you what it is worth, and stay with you through the financing and the close.

Restate the not-a-broker frame at the point of decision.

FAQ 4Q 5-12w · A 40-70w
Why does this need capital ready to invest?
Because this is about buying a real facility, not studying one, and that takes capital ready, the same as any property purchase. If your capital is ready, the plan we build with you can start now instead of a year from now. If it is not ready yet, the call still gives you a clear picture of what you are working toward, and you decide the timing, not us.

Reframe the capital gate as fit and timing, not a barrier.

Why this section

By now desire is set by the video and the proof; what remains is anxiety about the call itself. Answering the four real questions here lowers it so the ask lands.

Why this shape. A real accordion, closed by default, so the page stays short and a reader opens only what worries them. Four rows, not ten, so it reads as reassurance, not a wall.

Answers run 40 to 70 words: enough to actually resolve the fear, not a one-liner that reads as evasive. Questions stay short (5 to 12) because they are scan targets.

Rules that apply to every slot

  • One CTA label family. "START YOUR APPLICATION" at every page-level CTA position, including the sticky mobile bar. The application form's own step control ("Continue") is a form button, not part of the family.
  • Cleared proof only. Any name, quote or number that is not cleared stays out, and is never replaced with a plausible substitute.
  • No em dashes. House rule, every line.
  • Budgets are derived, not inherited. Raise one that does not fit; do not overrun it silently.
  • Do not pad to a minimum. A slot that does its job in fewer words has a wrong budget, not short copy.
How to read and use this wireframe optional

A wireframe locks structure, not words. It answers one question: is this laid out in the right order, with the right emphasis, to move this visitor to action?

Three views

Full preview is the page itself, at real type size on real page spacing. The view for judging whether the page works. Specs layers on the build detail: what each box is, how long it runs, what job it has. Persuasion opens a rail beside each section explaining why the section is there, why it is that shape, and why it runs that long.

Who uses which view

  • Reviewer: Full preview to judge the page, Persuasion to challenge it.
  • Copywriter: Specs to write, Persuasion to argue with a budget that does not fit the job.
  • Designer and developer: Specs. Layout, counts and fold budgets are the build contract.

Two conventions

  • Tags. SWAP changes per variant, CONSTANT is written once and reused, CUSTOMIZE V2 is constant now and hand-tuned later.
  • Amber means blocked. Something we genuinely do not have yet. Never filled with a plausible substitute.

The dark bar at the top, the view switch, the section summary and this block are all wireframe scaffolding. None of it exists on the real page.
