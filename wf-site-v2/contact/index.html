Before you book

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is custom, based on your unique business. We scope it after we understand your goals, your numbers and your team. There are no fixed packages.

Do you run our sales team?

No. We plug into your existing sales team, in‑house or outsourced, and feed it qualified calls. If you don't have a sales team yet, we can refer you to a partner who builds them.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We set targets with you, hold ourselves accountable to them and measure our work on your profit. Anyone guaranteeing results on paid traffic is guessing.

Who do you usually work with?

Mostly founder‑led expert brands in wealth and health that sell a high‑ticket offer on sales calls. We've also worked with agencies, law firms and other service businesses.

Who on our side works with you?

Usually the founder or CEO signs off, and a COO or head of operations works with us day to day. The founder stays involved: you're the magic of the business, and we build around you.

What if our offer is the problem?

Then we'll tell you. When ads stop working, the offer is often the real constraint. Sprint Testing is the fastest way to find out what the market wants now.
